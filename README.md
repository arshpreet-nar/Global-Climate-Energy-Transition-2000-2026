# Global Climate & Energy Transition Analysis (2000–2026)

## Project Overview

This project focuses on collecting and analyzing climate, energy, and environmental datasets from reliable secondary sources. The objective is to understand global trends in carbon emissions, renewable energy adoption, temperature changes, carbon pricing, and climate-related events.

## Datasets Used

1. co2_emissions_yearly.csv
2. energy_mix_yearly.csv
3. temperature_anomaly_monthly.csv
4. carbon_prices_daily.csv
5. climate_events.csv

## Data Type

Secondary Data

## File Formats

CSV (Comma-Separated Values)

## Project Objectives

* Analyze global CO₂ emission trends.
* Study renewable and fossil fuel energy consumption.
* Examine temperature anomalies and climate change indicators.
* Evaluate carbon market pricing trends.
* Investigate major climate-related events and disasters.

## Why These Data Sources Are Reliable

* Structured and well-organized datasets.
* Contain historical records from 2000 onwards.
* Suitable for data analytics, visualization, and research.
* Cover multiple aspects of climate and energy transition.
